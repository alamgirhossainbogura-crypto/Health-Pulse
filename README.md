 🩺 Health Pulse - AI-Powered Wellness Assistant

**Health Pulse** is a modern web application designed to simplify health tracking and dietary planning. Developed for the **#BuiltWithMeDo Hackathon**, it leverages AI to provide instant, actionable health insights.

---

### ✨ Key Features
* **Precision Health Metrics:** Instant calculation of **BMI** (Body Mass Index) and **BMR** (Basal Metabolic Rate).
* **AI Dietary Guides:** Specialized nutrition advice for **Diabetes, Hypertension, and Gastric issues**, powered by the **Google Gemini API**.
* **User-Centric Design:** A clean, responsive, and easy-to-navigate interface for all age groups.

### 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3 (Modern Flexbox/Grid Layout)
* **Logic:** JavaScript (ES6+)
* **AI Integration:** Google Gemini API
* **Platform:** MeDo Dev Environment

### 🚀 How it Works
1. **Input Metrics:** Enter your weight and height to get your physical status.
2. **Select Category:** Click on specific health cards (e.g., Diabetes Guide).
3. **Get AI Insights:** The app fetches real-time dietary recommendations tailored to your selected condition.
