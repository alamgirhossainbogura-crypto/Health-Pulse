Health Pulse is an AI-powered health assistant built for the #BuiltWithMeDo Hackathon. ​🩺 Health Pulse - AI-Powered Wellness Assistant
​Health Pulse is a modern web application designed to simplify health tracking and dietary planning. Developed for the #BuiltWithMeDo Hackathon, it leverages the power of AI to provide instant, actionable health insights.
​✨ Key Features
​Precision Health Metrics: Instant calculation of BMI (Body Mass Index) and BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate).
​AI Dietary Guides: Specialized nutrition advice for Diabetes, Hypertension, and Gastric issues powered by Google Gemini API.
​User-Centric Design: A clean, responsive, and easy-to-navigate interface for all age groups.
​🛠️ Tech Stack
​Frontend: HTML5, CSS3 (Modern Flexbox/Grid Layout)
​Logic: JavaScript (ES6+)
​AI Integration: Google Gemini API
​Platform: MeDo Dev Environment
​🚀 How it Works
​Input Metrics: Enter your weight and height to get your physical status.
​Select Category: Click on specific health cards (e.g., Diabetes Guide).
​Get AI Insights: The app fetches real-time dietary recommendations tailored to your selected condition.
